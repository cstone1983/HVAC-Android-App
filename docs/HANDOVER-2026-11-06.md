## Behind it

- **n8n:** one CONDITIONS table plus `zoneSched` / `zoneBoost` / `zoneTarget`, pasted into four places:
  - Controller: Compute Sequencer Actions.
  - Watchdog: Evaluate. Its new `condSettling` gives a zone 3 min after a covering condition changes.
  - Bot: Check Verify, and Handle (its +/- buttons edit the block in use).
  - In the sequencer, a condition change only moves temperatures on running, in-use heads.
  - Init Run accepts `input_boolean.hvac_*` and `timer.hvac_*`.
- **Home Assistant:**
  - The forwarder has a `condition` trigger (2 s debounce, resent 25 s later).
  - New automations: `HVAC - Night delay finished` and `HVAC - House conditions housekeeping`.
  - `Night - Phone Charging Shutdown Trigger` now skips the shutdown once when the flag is set.
- **App:** the rules are in `model/HouseConditions.kt` and the page in `ui/HeatPumpSettings.kt`. Everything is
  configured by `hvacSettings` in layout_config.json: wording, minutes, durations, scopes, helpers. Adding a
  condition means a helper, a config entry and a row in the n8n table (all four copies), with no reinstall.
- **Verification:**
  - 112 n8n cases, 8 bot verify cases and 5 bot handle cases in `tools/n8n-logic-tests`.
  - 147 app unit tests, including the new `HouseConditionsTest`.
  - An independent logic review; its findings were fixed, except Anthony's 64.
  - Live, with the house Off:
    - Each helper reached n8n and gave a no-op.
    - The 10 s Night countdown turned on Night early.
    - Kids away was toggled from the kitchen tablet.
    - No n8n errors.
  - Not yet tested with the house heating: Boost and Kids away actually moving heads.
